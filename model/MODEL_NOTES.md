## Two Direct Lake constraints that shaped every measure

**1. No calculated columns, no calculated tables.** Adding either drops the model
out of Direct Lake into DirectQuery fallback — which would quietly void component
7's entire claim ("a semantic model with no Gold notebook underneath it"). Two
consequences, both visible in `measures.py`:

- There is no `_Measures` table. All 24 measures hang off `alerts` and are
  organised by `displayFolder`.
- Alert grading could not be a calculated column. It is done in KQL instead
  (`02_KQL/12_alert_grading.kql`), which is also where the contract says the
  transformation layer belongs — the constraint and the architecture agree.

**2. Measures should reduce to column scans.** Interval-overlap logic against a
disconnected `ground_truth` table, evaluated per visual per refresh, is the
pattern Direct Lake handles worst. Pushing the overlap into KQL turns
`Precision (Ground Truth)` into two `COUNTROWS` calls.

**9 of the 24 are marked `pending_dependency`** — they reference the two missing

### `Precision (Reportable)` is the one to read carefully

Every other precision measure is unguarded and must **not** be bound to a visual.
`Precision (Reportable)` returns `BLANK()` unless exactly one cohort is in filter
context *and* that cohort is `graded_T90_T60`.

Blank rather than zero is the entire point. Zero renders as a confident wrong bar;
blank renders as an empty cell the visual labels "pending", which is what contract
§5 requires — *"the T-30 → T and live cohorts shown as awaiting grading, not as
zero"*. `Cohort Status` supplies that label. Together they make blending a cohort
structurally impossible rather than a reviewer's job to notice.

Note what this means for Session 6's page today: **`card_testing_burst` is the
only reflex with any graded rows at all** (29 in `graded_T90_T60`). The other
three sit entirely in `awaiting_grading_T30_T` because of the retried `as_of`
values Session 4 had to use. The page will therefore show one real precision
number and three "awaiting grading" rows — and that is the honest picture, not a
half-finished one.

## Recall has no per-cohort denominator

`Recall (Ground Truth)` is reported **by reflex type, never by maturity cohort**.
A maturity cohort is a property of an alert's age. A planted ground-truth episode
has no cohort. Slicing recall by cohort would put a cohort-filtered numerator over
an unfiltered denominator and produce a number that looks precise and means
nothing. Recorded here so that no page invents one later.
